MODIS GPP Super-Resolution with Sentinel-2

A two-stage framework for enhancing MODIS Gross Primary Production (GPP) from 500 m to 20 m using Sentinel-2 vegetation information and environmental variables, without requiring spatially dense high-resolution GPP ground truth.

Research Overview Public 🔒 Methodology Restricted MODIS GPP Sentinel-2 500 m → 20 m Super-Resolution
01 · Overview

From coarse MODIS GPP to spatially detailed 20 m estimation

Gross Primary Production (GPP) is widely used to characterize ecosystem carbon uptake, but MODIS GPP is available at a relatively coarse 500 m spatial resolution. This study investigates whether Sentinel-2 vegetation information and environmental variables can reconstruct spatially detailed GPP at 20 m without requiring a spatially complete high-resolution GPP target.

Study area and data. MODIS GPP at 500 m, Sentinel-2 observations at 20 m, and daily flux-tower GPP are evaluated across Minnesota and California.
02 · Key Contributions

Turning sparse supervision into spatially detailed carbon-uptake estimates

Unlike conventional image super-resolution, fine-resolution GPP does not have a spatially dense ground-truth target. This research addresses the resulting learning and validation challenge by connecting coarse MODIS productivity estimates with fine-scale Sentinel-2 observations and ecological knowledge.

01

Super-resolution without dense 20 m GPP ground truth

Formulates GPP downscaling as an estimation problem constrained by coarse productivity information and fine-resolution environmental observations, rather than relying on unavailable 20 m GPP labels.

Limited-label Learning
02

Cross-resolution reference-guided learning

Introduces a progressive auxiliary-to-main learning framework designed to transfer vegetation-related fine-scale structure to GPP reconstruction while retaining information from observed MODIS GPP.

Cross-scale Learning
03

Ecologically constrained spatial reconstruction

Integrates coarse-scale consistency and vegetation–productivity relationships to support plausible spatial patterns, rather than optimizing image sharpness alone.

Physical Relevance
03 · Approach at a Glance

Bridging the 500 m-to-20 m resolution gap

The approach combines MODIS GPP with Sentinel-2 vegetation information and environmental context to estimate 20 m productivity patterns. Its central research contribution is learning and evaluating spatial detail when pixel-wise 20 m GPP ground truth is unavailable. The architecture, supervisory relationships, and specific training objectives are restricted to the detailed methodology.

04 · Minnesota Results

Improved agreement with flux-tower GPP in Minnesota

Independent validation uses three flux towers: US-Ro4, US-Ro5, and US-Ro6. Among the compared estimates, PR-Aux provides the strongest aggregate performance, while PR-Main also improves the overall relationship relative to the original MODIS GPP.

PR-Aux performs best in the regional aggregate

PR-Aux achieves RMSE 27.7095 and R² 0.6420, compared with RMSE 29.6649 and R² 0.5332 for MODIS GPP. The presentation reports a 6.59% reduction in RMSE and a 20.41% improvement in R² relative to MODIS GPP.

RMSE 27.71 · Validation across 3 flux towers

Minnesota validation. Aggregate comparison across US-Ro4, US-Ro5, and US-Ro6. PR-Aux has the lowest RMSE and highest R² among the evaluated methods.
05 · California Results

Stronger regional performance from the main super-resolution model

California validation uses seven flux-tower sites: US-Bi1, US-Bi2, US-Ton, US-Tw1, US-Tw4, US-Tw5, and US-Var. In this region, PR-Main provides the strongest aggregate performance among the evaluated methods.

PR-Main reduces error relative to MODIS GPP

PR-Main achieves RMSE 30.5925 and R² 0.5720, compared with RMSE 39.2453 and R² 0.5471 for MODIS GPP. The presentation reports a 22.04% reduction in RMSE and a 4.55% improvement in R² relative to MODIS GPP.

06 · Spatial Analysis

Recovering fine-scale GPP patterns while constraining unrealistic values

The spatial analysis focuses on limitations of coarse MODIS GPP, including missing or weakly represented productivity patterns in non-vegetated and heterogeneous areas. High-resolution Sentinel-2 reference information enables finer spatial variation, while the ecological relationship loss discourages implausible GPP values.

Qualitative spatial comparison. The proposed model reconstructs spatially detailed 20 m GPP patterns using Sentinel-2 reference information while maintaining coarse-scale and ecological constraints.
