MODIS GPP Super-Resolution with Sentinel-2

A two-stage framework for enhancing MODIS Gross Primary Production (GPP) from 500 m to 20 m using Sentinel-2 vegetation information and environmental variables, without requiring spatially dense high-resolution GPP ground truth.

MODIS GPP Sentinel-2 500 m → 20 m Super-Resolution
01 · Overview

High-resolution mapping of ecosystem productivity

GPP represents the amount of carbon fixed by vegetation through photosynthesis and is a key variable for monitoring terrestrial carbon dynamics. MODIS provides widely used GPP products, but their 500 m spatial resolution limits fine-scale analysis. This study reconstructs GPP at 20 m by combining MODIS GPP with Sentinel-2 observations and auxiliary environmental information.

02 · Motivation

Learning without dense 20 m GPP ground truth

Directly measured GPP is available only at a limited number of flux towers, making dense supervised training difficult. In addition, relationships learned at 500 m do not necessarily transfer to 20 m, where local extremes and fine-scale spatial variability can be lost through aggregation.

01

No dense high-resolution GPP labels

The framework avoids relying on sparse flux-tower measurements as spatially continuous training targets, and instead uses coarse MODIS GPP together with high-resolution proxy relationships.

Weak Supervision
02

Multi-scale ecological guidance

Sentinel-2 vegetation indices provide 20 m spatial structure, while MODIS GPP constrains the overall GPP magnitude at 500 m.

Cross-Scale Learning
03

Ecologically informed constraints

Training incorporates vegetation–GPP relationships so that predictions remain consistent with expected productivity patterns, especially in low- and high-vegetation regimes.

Domain Knowledge
03 · Method

Auxiliary-to-main super-resolution framework

Training is divided into an auxiliary regression stage and a main super-resolution stage. The auxiliary model first learns a high-resolution GPP representation from environmental variables. The main model then learns to recover 20 m GPP from low-resolution GPP while preserving the spatial relationships captured by the auxiliary model.

Prepare multi-source inputs

MODIS GPP is provided at 500 m, while Sentinel-2 bands and vegetation indices are represented at 20 m. Additional variables include aspect, date, minimum temperature, and maximum temperature.

Train PR-Aux

PR-Aux predicts 20 m GPP without using MODIS GPP as a direct input. Its output is constrained by 500 m MODIS GPP after downsampling and by high-resolution relationships with EVI and NIRv.

Train and infer with PR-Main

PR-Main receives low-resolution GPP and auxiliary variables, learns against the MODIS-scale value constraint and PR-Aux spatial correlation, and directly produces 20 m GPP during inference.

04 · Learning

Losses that connect coarse values and fine spatial patterns

The objective combines scale-consistency, spatial dependency, and vegetation–productivity constraints. This allows the models to learn fine-resolution patterns while remaining consistent with MODIS GPP.

L1

Resize Loss

The predicted 20 m GPP is downsampled and compared with 500 m MODIS GPP to preserve coarse-scale GPP values.

Value Consistency
L2

Correlation Loss

Distance correlation is used to encourage dependency between predicted GPP and high-resolution reference variables. The loss is defined as 1 − Distance Correlation.

Spatial Dependency
L3

EVI–GPP Relationship Loss

Additional penalties discourage implausibly low GPP in highly vegetated areas and non-zero GPP in very low-EVI non-water regions.

Ecological Prior
05 · Results

Improved spatial detail and flux-tower agreement

The proposed PR-Aux and PR-Main models generally outperform Random Forest and SE-ResNet baselines. Performance varies by site, but the framework better reproduces GPP variability and reduces the strong underestimation observed in challenging California conditions.

Where the method helps most

In the California US-Bi1 validation site, MODIS GPP shows substantial underestimation, while PR-Main reduces RMSE from 41.59 to 28.18. Qualitative comparisons also show that the proposed models recover finer spatial structure and reduce extreme artifacts while preserving GPP increase/decrease patterns.
